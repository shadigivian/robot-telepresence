# Test coverage

Executed: 58 Vitest unit/domain tests; 13 Playwright Chrome tests covering Owner/Admin/User invitation and return, navigation, scoped routes, settings/maps persistence, reset, sessions/control, media-denial paths, kiosk and layout checks; two static deployment-base checks.

The layout test covers the seven seeded screen types at its configured 1440×1000 viewport and Surface landscape 1368×912, Surface portrait 912×1368, and mobile 390×844 for kiosk/operations. The requested 1920×1080, 1440×900, and 768×1024 profiles were not separately verified. Firefox/WebKit were not run. Browser screenshots generated by the project’s visual test are in `audit-reports/screenshots/` (13 files). Intermediate failure traces remain in ignored `test-results/` and are not presented as final failures.

Not exhaustively covered: all CRUD and negative cases listed in the brief, multi-tab races beyond the existing session test, storage fault matrix, visual reference comparison, and physical hardware. Refer to `docs/FEATURE_CHECKLIST.md` for intended features; it is not evidence that every workflow was executed in this audit.
